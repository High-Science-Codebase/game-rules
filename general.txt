<font size="25"><b>1. Do not be toxic</b></font>

  <b>a.</b> Do not insult, provoke, or spam other players.
  <b>b.</b> Do not start arguments or try to make others upset on purpose.


<font size="25"><b>2. Do not act inappropriately</b></font>


<font size="25"><b>3. Do not harass other players</b></font>

  <b>a.</b> Do not say mean or hurtful things to other players.
  <b>b.</b> Do not repeatedly bother, target or follow a player.


<font size="25"><b>4. Do not bypass chat filters</b></font>

  <b>a.</b> Do not use spacing, symbols, misspellings, or other tricks to get around the chat filter.
  <b>b.</b> Intent does not matter. Bypassing the filter is a violation regardless of the reason.


<font size="25"><b>6. Do not glitch or abuse bugs</b></font>

  <b>a.</b> Do not intentionally use bugs or glitches for an unfair advantage.
  <b>b.</b> Failing to report a discovered glitch or bug may result in a punishment.


<font size="25"><b>7. Do not exploit</b></font>


<font color="rgb(255,200,0)">⚠️ <b>Any violation of these rules will result in a warning or restriction of your access to the game.</b> For appeals, visit the community server.</font>
